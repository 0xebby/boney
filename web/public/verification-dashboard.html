The layers

Five readers, one of them independent

Claim

userCreditedOf — what the project reported and the chain credited, segmented per action block.

blind to whether it was true
Ceiling

observedProgressOf — what the relayer saw in the logs, unfiltered. Gated KPIs only.

blind to the KPI's topic filter, and absent on 9 of 15 KPIs
Subgraph

Credit and TierPayout folded — the whole credited history, in two POSTs instead of 45 calls.

blind to the verifier: no data source watches it
Re-scan

eth_getLogs replayed against each KPI's own params, narrowed to that campaign's referrals.

the only column not produced by the code it checks
Escrow

paidOut and remainingPool — money that actually moved.

blind to which KPI earned it

Checkpoints

Where the relayer stopped, and what that leaves unobserved

lastScannedBlock is keyed (campaign, kpi, epoch) and lives on chain, because the relayer process keeps no state. Totals are additive — a batch writes stored + delta — so re-folding a range that is already behind the checkpoint counts it twice. That is why the resume point is checkpoint + 1 and why a rescan is refused rather than clamped.
