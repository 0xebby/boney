Method
Every count and payout is totalProgress, progressOf, settledTiersOf and paidOut read straight off the campaign contract, then cross-checked against the Boneyard subgraph’s 81 independent credit events. The two agree exactly, to the credit, and the settled rungs add up to the 450,000 bUSD the contract says it has paid.
How a credit lands
Only the project or the oracle coordinator may report, and the contract credits nothing but the increase over a wallet’s last total — a replay is a no-op and a lower total reverts. Which promoter earns it is resolved against the attribution registry at report time, and a wallet that changed promoter mid-span is refused rather than guessed at.
